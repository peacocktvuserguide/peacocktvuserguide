Why You May Need to Login to Peacock TV on Smart TV
---------------------------------------------------

You may need to log in to Peacock TV on your Smart TV if you:

* Already have a Peacock account.
* Want to watch Peacock on your television.
* Have recently installed the Peacock app.
* Purchased a Peacock subscription.
* Signed out of your Peacock account.
* Switched to a new Smart TV.
* Need to reconnect your Peacock account.

How to Install Peacock TV on a Smart TV
---------------------------------------

Turn on your Smart TV → Open the TV's app store → Search for **Peacock** → Select the official Peacock app → Choose **Install** or **Download** → Wait for the installation to finish → Open the Peacock app.

How to Login to Peacock TV on Smart TV
--------------------------------------

Open the Peacock app on your Smart TV → Select **Sign In** → Follow the instructions displayed on the screen → Enter your Peacock email address and password if requested → Complete the sign-in process → Wait for your account to load → Start watching Peacock TV.

How to Login Using an Activation Code
-------------------------------------

Some Smart TVs may display an activation code when you choose **Sign In**.

Open the Peacock app → Select **Sign In** → Look for the activation code displayed on your TV → Use a phone, tablet, or computer to open the official Peacock activation page → Enter the code shown on your Smart TV → Sign in to your Peacock account if requested → Follow the instructions to activate your TV → Return to the Peacock app on your Smart TV.

Tips for Logging In to Peacock on Smart TV
------------------------------------------

* Make sure your Smart TV is connected to the internet.
* Use the email address associated with your Peacock account.
* Enter your password carefully.
* Make sure the Peacock app is updated.
* Keep your Smart TV software updated.
* Use the official Peacock app.
* Do not share your Peacock account password with others.

What to Do If Peacock Won't Let You Login
-----------------------------------------

* Check your Smart TV's internet connection.
* Restart the Peacock app.
* Restart your Smart TV.
* Check your email address and password.
* Update the Peacock app.
* Update your Smart TV software.
* Try signing in again after a few minutes.
* Reset your Peacock password if you have forgotten it.

How to Fix Peacock Activation Code Problems
-------------------------------------------

If the activation code does not work, close and reopen the Peacock app on your Smart TV to generate a new code.

Enter the new code exactly as it appears on your TV. If the problem continues, check your internet connection, restart the Smart TV, update the Peacock app, and try the activation process again.

How to Reset Your Peacock Password
----------------------------------

If you have forgotten your Peacock password, select the password recovery option on the Peacock sign-in page.

Enter the email address associated with your Peacock account → Submit the password reset request → Check your email inbox → Open the password reset message → Follow the instructions → Create a new password → Return to your Smart TV → Open Peacock → Sign in using your new password.

Common Peacock Smart TV Login Problems

* Activation code is not working.
* Activation code has expired.
